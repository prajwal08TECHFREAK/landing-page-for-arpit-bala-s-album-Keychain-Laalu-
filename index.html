the record

an album about lost things — and the stories they keep for us.

A keychain, a lighter, a letter folded into a drawer. Keychain Laalu treats every small leftover as an archive: the object outlives the relationship, and if it ever crosses your path again, it drags the whole story back with it.

Bala built the record across a year of livestream snippets and late studio sessions, then played it end to end on YouTube the night before release. Two singles led the way — Pyari Amaanat and Kaise Manaye.

Warm, unbothered production from Adil, Angad Virk, A.O.D. and Karan Kanchan, with Natiq, Toorjo Dey and NEVERSOBER filling in the corners. Every song ships with its instrumental, so the mood is yours to borrow.
